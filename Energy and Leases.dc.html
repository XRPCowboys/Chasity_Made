ENERGY & MINERAL LEASES

Automating Production Splits: Physical Flow-Meter Integration

Landowners, mineral lease managers, and fractional trust beneficiaries are routinely plagued by manual accounting discrepancies, paper check delays, and opaque royalty reporting from legacy oil and gas producers. Over half of all oil and gas royalty payments are still issued via physical paper checks, creating massive administrative costs, mailing delays, and constant reconciliation disputes. SSR completely modernizes resource accounting by integrating physical flow-meters directly with digital automated settlement rails.

How Payout Routing Works

Direct Hardware Integration

The platform integrates directly with physical industrial flow-meters at the wellhead or refinery via secure, real-time APIs.

Zero-Error Split Sheets

The system pulls the real-time production flow and sales data, automatically mapping the exact contract split-sheets (e.g., 35% to the Landowner / 15% to the Lease Operator / 15% to the Backers) without human intervention.

Frictionless Clearing

Converted Secure Digital Dollars route directly to stakeholder digital accounts in seconds, completely bypassing bank holidays and postal delays.

Proven Industry Case Study: Zavanna Energy

This technology has been rigorously tested in the field by independent operators like Zavanna Energy LLC, which manages over 125 active oil and gas wells in North Dakota and Wyoming. Running our automated platform in parallel with their legacy manual reporting systems, Zavanna cut their overall royalty check-processing costs exactly in half, while receiving overwhelming praise from mineral owners for providing absolute, paperless transparency and security.

Unassailable Audit Trails

Raw geological reports, land deeds, and production data are anchored directly to the immutable ledger with a Tamper-Proof Stamp. This creates an unalterable, permanently synchronized database, giving joint venture partners and tax authorities instant, unassailable audit capabilities.
