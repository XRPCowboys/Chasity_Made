SPLIT SECOND ROYALTIES LLC — SOFTWARE LICENSE & TERMS OF PLATFORM SERVICES

Document ID: SSR-TERMS-2026-V9.0  |  Effective Date: August 20, 2026  |  Read the full Terms →

These Software License & Terms of Administration ("Terms") govern your use of the SplitSecond Royalties LLC ("Company," "we," or "us") administrative, tokenization, and payout software tools. By creating an account, registering your assets, and utilizing our white-label business portals, you ("Client" or "you") agree to be bound by these Terms.

1. Scope of Administration Services

SplitSecond Royalties provides pre-engineered software application solutions to automate the ingestion, matching, and distribution of intellectual property, mineral, and trademark royalties. Client may license our software, configure custom payout split sheets, and monitor cleared royalties in real-time.

2. Zero Upfront Setup Costs

There is no cost to register your account or onboard your portfolios onto our platform. The Company does not charge upfront setup, database, or software licensing fees directly to B2B Operating Partners or Rights Owners who license our software. Platform operations are funded on autopilot through integrated, ledger-level transaction schedules configured by the licensee.

3. Redirection of Revenues & Letters of Direction (LOD)

To activate your automated payout distributions, you must execute a legally binding Letter of Direction (LOD) authorizing the redirection of your gross revenue payments from your current distributors, performance rights organizations, or operating clearing houses directly to the Company's designated clearing coordinates. The Company cannot initiate payout distributions until cleared funds are received at our gateways.

4. Payout Distribution Waterfalls

4.1 Master Sound Recording & Physical Asset Net Splits — The software tool provides a customizable split-sheet and automated waterfall system. For convenience, the software is pre-loaded with a standard, default template that licensees can choose to apply, allocating cleared master and physical revenues received at their secure coordinates and instantly routing them through native ledger primitives to secure wallets as follows:

  • The Owner / Rights Holder: 35% Net Split (Configurable default).
  • The B2B Partner (Operating/Recording Studio): 15% Net Split (Configurable default).
  • Split Second Royalties Software Licensing Fee: 38% Net Split (Standard default for utilizing our software and technology).
  • The Client-Managed Project Support Pool: 12% Net Split (Configurable default. If the licensee chooses to raise capital from independent project supporters, the software enables them to programmatically route this split on-chain directly to active project supporter wallets, with 100% distributed to verified supporters).

4.2 Composition (Publishing) Administration splits — For underlying musical work copyrights, the software tool includes a standard default template under which the Client may designate the Company to act as its Publishing Administrator. Under this arrangement:

  • The Company takes zero copyright ownership and acts solely as an administrative collection and metadata-matching agent.
  • The songwriter/creator receives exactly 80% of all collected mechanical and performance royalties directly to their account.
  • The Company retains a flat 20% publishing administration fee of composition revenues to cover registrations, matching metadata, and global collections.

5. Technical Ingestion, Hashing, and Storage

White-Glove Setup: Upon receipt of your metadata and standard spreadsheets, our delivery team will encrypt your files with AES-256 and anchor their unique SHA-256 cryptographic hashes directly to the XRP Ledger within 48 hours to prove unalterable provenance of your legal rights.

Underwriting Filter: Under rolling software policies, the licensed software integrates an automated filter that screens out and excludes non-performing, under-threshold assets (specifically, music tracks averaging under 1,000 rolling annual streams) to protect capital stability.

6. Active Wallet Operations & Transaction Fees

Client accounts are provisioned with secure, non-custodial Multi-Party Computation (MPC) wallets. Client retains full ownership and control over their cleared royalties, with the ability to sweep funds, execute peer-to-peer transfers, and route custom splits to collaborators. To maintain system velocity, the platform features a flat volume fee of 5 basis points (0.05%) on asset creation, contract alignments, and secondary trades, which is passed through directly to W3SaaS to cover transaction execution costs on the white-label rails.

7. Account Termination and Offboarding

You may terminate your account at any time upon 30 days' written notice. Upon termination, you must submit a counter-LOD to your distributors to redirect your cash flows back to your personal coordinates. Within 30 days of termination, we will provide you with all decrypted files and historical transaction logs in a simple, standardized text format, programmatically close your sub-tenancy portals, and deactivate your split sheets.

8. Automated Legal Document & SEC Compliance Processing

To help our licensees manage their legal and compliance requirements, the licensed software features built-in compliance and legal document processing tools that licensees can utilize directly in their onboarding workflow:

  • Tier A Corporate SAFE Automation: For the Company's own corporate-level seed round, the system programmatically generates a Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE) drawn from the 1,000,000-share Strategic Corporate Reserve, pre-set at a $20,000,000 valuation cap, and routes it directly to the seed project supporter via a secure white-labeled e-signing API.
  • Tier B Project PPM & Subscription Automation: For licensees who choose to secure project project supporters, the portal programmatically compiles customized, asset-specific Private Placement Memorandums (PPMs) and Subscription Agreements, dynamically injecting specific asset identifiers (such as ISRCs, ISWCs, or mineral tract numbers) and localized risk factors prior to on-chain split execution.
  • Automated Form D Reporting: On the exact Date of First Sale of any offering pool, the software tool automatically compiles all required SEC Form D metadata and exports a pre-populated Form D Draft Worksheet (PDF/XML) for the licensee to safeguard the 15-calendar-day SEC filing deadline and prevent EDGAR portal session timeouts.
  • On-Chain Compliance Screening: The platform's compliance middleware enforces integrated identity verification (KYC/KYB) and real-time screening against the U.S. OFAC Specially Designated Nationals registry, programmatically blocking wallet provisioning or payout routing for any flagged entities.
