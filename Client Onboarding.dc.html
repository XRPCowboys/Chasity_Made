CLIENT PORTAL ONBOARDING

Digital Client Onboarding & Legal Flow

Register your company, sign your Letter of Direction, and go live entirely inside the portal.

The Registration Flow

1
Account Registration

The B2B Client (Recording Studio, Artist, Landowner, or Brand Licensor) enters their company name, contact details, and primary administrator login.

2
Digital Consent (Click-wrap)

Before submitting registration, the Client must check a mandatory box confirming they've read and agree to the Platform Terms of Administration, rendered in a scrollable, read-only container.

3
Catalog/Asset Ingestion

Once logged in, the Client drags and drops their metadata spreadsheets, asset logs, or lease documents into the Smart Ingestion Bridge.

4
Automated LOD Generation & E-Sign

The portal programmatically populates a customized Letter of Direction from the Client's registered data, executed with a single-click digital signature.
